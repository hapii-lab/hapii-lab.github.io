about hapii

The human-centered, adaptive, and personalized information interaction lab at Cal Poly Pomona aims to make users happier, by researching novel computing solutions that understand and support each individual person. Our expertise ranges across several areas, including:

Human-Centered AI
Multilingual Computing
Data Visualization

hapii projects

Current research project

Multilingual STEM

Multilingual learners can face barriers in STEM when academic language, rather than conceptual understanding, limits access to learning. This research explores human-centered multilingual technologies that combine adaptive interfaces, multimodal support, and AI-driven personalization to help students learn across languages while supporting teachers, tutors, and families.

Current research project

Student Success Dashboards

In collaboration with the Student Success Strategic Initiatives team at the CSU Office of the Chancellor, the hapii lab works on dashboards that help faculty, staff, and administrators understand student progress, identify trends, and surface equity gaps. This research explores AI-driven visualization and interaction techniques that make dashboards more adaptive and explainable, helping users discover patterns and turn data into student-support decisions.

Current research project

AI-Enhanced Language Assessment

This research explores how eye tracking can capture real-time language processing during online assessments, and how machine learning can combine gaze behavior with test performance to better understand language proficiency and academic readiness. The goal is to develop scalable, human-centered assessment methods that augment traditional scores with cognitive evidence while prioritizing validity, privacy, fairness, and interpretability.
