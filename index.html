about hapii

The human-centered, adaptive, and personalized information interaction lab at Cal Poly Pomona aims to make users happier, by researching novel computing solutions that understand and support each individual person. Our expertise ranges across several areas, including:

Human-Centered AI
Multilingual Computing
Data Visualization

hapii projects

Current research project

Multilingual STEM

Multilingual learners can encounter barriers in STEM when academic language, rather than conceptual understanding, restricts their access to learning and full participation. This research investigates human-centered multilingual technologies that integrate adaptive interfaces, multimodal resources, and AI-driven personalization to help students navigate complex concepts and learning materials across languages, while offering valuable tools and guidance to teachers, tutors, and families throughout the learning process.

Sample publications Math-Language Minigames Homework Support for Bilingual Students
Current research project

Student Success Dashboards

In collaboration with the Student Success Strategic Initiatives team at the CSU Office of the Chancellor, the hapii lab develops dashboards that help faculty, staff, and administrators understand student progress, identify meaningful trends, and surface equity gaps. This research explores AI-driven visualization and interaction techniques that make dashboards more adaptive and explainable, helping users discover patterns and turn complex data into informed student-support decisions.

Related Links Student Success Strategic Initiatives
Current research project

AI-Enhanced Language Assessment

This research investigates how eye tracking can complement traditional language assessments by capturing how test takers process language in real time. By combining gaze behavior, test performance, and measures of academic attainment, the project will develop machine-learning models to better understand language proficiency and predict academic success. The goal is to provide scalable cognitive evidence that strengthens assessment validity while protecting test-taker privacy.

Past research project

Personalized Multilingual Information Access

This research investigated how multilingual users search for, select, and consume information across languages. It examined multilingual information seeking across desktop and mobile interfaces, including whether eye-gaze behavior can be used to infer a search user’s language proficiency, as well as methods for overcoming language silos in recommender systems. The work seeks to design adaptive information access systems that better support multilingual users’ language preferences, needs, and search behaviors.

Sample publications How Do Multilingual Users Search? Multilingual News
Past research project

Adaptive Information Visualization

This research explored how information visualizations can adapt to individual differences in users’ characteristics, abilities, and preferences. It investigated interactive visualization aids and used eye-tracking data with computational modeling to infer human factors that influence how users process visual information during complex visual analysis tasks. The findings support adaptive, personalized visualization systems that provide appropriate assistance based on each user’s characteristics and behavior.

Sample publications Towards Adaptive Information Visualization Computational Methods to Infer Human Factors
Past research project

Misleading Visualizations and Human-AI Synergy

This research investigated why people are susceptible to misleading data visualizations and how AI can reduce misinterpretation. It examined how visualization literacy, user characteristics, graph design, and device context affect comprehension, while comparing human and AI performance to determine when personalized assistance is most valuable. The work advances context-aware support that helps users recognize manipulation and interpret visual evidence accurately and confidently.

Sample publication Humans and AI against Misleading Information Visualizations (in press)
