Overview & purpose

Two intertwined fields.
One polytechnic experience.

The minor bridges computer science and linguistics in the design and deployment of technology that understands, processes, and generates human language.

01

Built for an AI-driven workforce

Students gain mathematical, quantitative, algorithmic, and programming skills through a feasible program that can complement any major.

02

Grounded in language science

Students examine the science and systematicity of language use across domains, not just model outputs at the surface level.

03

Designed around applied research

A hands-on culminating project gives students the opportunity to integrate computational and linguistic methods.

04

Connected across colleges

Students gain a cross-college experience and access to cutting-edge equipment through the hapii lab, LaLaLab, and more.

CPP advantage

Interdisciplinary by design

The minor builds on CPP’s strengths in Computer Science and Applied Linguistics, as well as demonstrated student interest in interdisciplinary data-focused programs.

Technical advantage

Combine analytical language training with algorithmic thinking, and evaluate model outputs beyond surface-level metrics.

Communication advantage

Translate between engineers and domain experts while designing stronger annotation guidelines and linguistic datasets.

Career advantage
